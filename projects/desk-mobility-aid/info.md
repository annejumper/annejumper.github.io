My final Engineering Design I user-centered project challenged my team to design a desk for a Middle School teacher with cerebral palsy who uses his desk as a mobility aid. Features of our desk include a wooden table top with grooved edges to prevent items from rolling off, cup holders to hold drinks of multiple sizes or pencils, a mug holder, a chair to put a plush dog memorializing the user's former guide dog, holders for walking sticks, a tray to hold paper, and a sliding handle that helps the user walk with the desk as a mobility aid.
