For this project for my Mechanics I class, we were tasked with designing a statically determinate truss, calculating theoretical support reactions and internal member forces, and then validating those results experimentally and computationally. We achieved this by designing and constructing a symmetric gambrel truss, first verifying determinacy. The truss was analyzed under two loading conditions: a 200 g load applied at the center joint and a 200 g load applied at the left joint. Support reactions were measured experimentally using scales and compared to theoretical calculations, followed by member force analysis using equilibrium methods. Finally, the results were checked using an online truss calculator and MATLAB to confirm consistency across all approaches.
