I have made parts using a variety of manufacturing techniques and machinery found in [Bray](https://sites.tufts.edu/bray/) (Tufts’ machine shop) and [Nolop](https://nolop.org/) (Tufts’ maker space). I was also trained on much of this machinery in Materials and Manufacturing I. I have fabrication experience in laser cutting, 3D printing, sand casting, injection molding, strip heating, metalworking (sheet metal forming, water jet cutting, vertical and horizontal band saws, drill press, lathe, mill), woodworking (table saw, miter saw, drill press, jig saw), and more.
