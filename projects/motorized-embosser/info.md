For my Engineering Design I class, we were tasked with designing a motorized embossing mechanism that could complete a sequence of three consecutive stamps on a piece of aluminum foil with dies that were interchangeable. We used a CAM mechanism run by a 12 V DC motor to maximize the force with which the die is stamped and constructed our mechanism using laser cut wood and acrylic, dowels, and 3D printed custom dies.
