For the final project of my Thermal Fluid Systems II class, we were tasked with using our analytical and modeling knowledge to design a solution to a real-world problem. We chose to design a low-cost, water-efficient irrigation system for a Zambian eggplant field to help relieve the effects of the country's drought. We created a design with redundancy and performed calculations using Bernoulli's equation and head loss to determine features needed for each plant to get the required volume of water. We used a simplified COMSOL laminar flow simulation to analyze the velocity and pressure changes within a segment of drip tape in the irrigation system to compare to our analytical results.
