This project was combined between my Mechanics I and Materials and Manufacturing I classes. For the Materials and Manufacturing I component, we were tasked with using a static FEA simulation in SolidWorks to analyze the normal stress and deflection in a steel pull up bar subjected to a concentrated load in the middle. Then for the Mechanics I component, we determined the normal stress and deflection values theoretically and compared the results to the FEA simulation.
