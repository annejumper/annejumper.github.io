For this project for my Electronics & Controls I class, we were tasked with building an electromechanical game that is controlled by a KB2040 microcontroller, takes input from a player, and utilizes an H-bridge which drives either a stepper or DC motor to move a part of the game. I built a tilt maze controlled by two DC motors and two H-bridges that takes user input from a joystick. The goal is to tilt the maze to move a ball along a pre-determined path without falling into any of the holes.
