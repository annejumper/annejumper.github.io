For my final project for my Engineering Design II class, we were assigned a product (an electric ratchet wrench in our case) and tasked with tearing it down. Three components were selected (the bearings, planetary gearbox, and the ratcheting mechanism) and each component was analyzed using a SolidWorks FEA analysis to determine the adequacy of the design. I specifically analyzed the planetary gearbox and conducted calculations to determine the torque ratio and tooth force and conducted an FEA analysis to compute the teeth stresses under worst-case loading conditions and determined the factor of safety.
