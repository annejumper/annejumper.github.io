For this project for my Engineering Design I class, we were assigned a user product (a Wilton Cookie Press in our case) and tasked with tearing it down. Each component was modeled in SolidWorks to make an assembly and exploded view of the product and engineering drawings of two components. Additionally, the function of the product is analyzed with a "How it Works" video and a functional decomposition and the parts are organized in a Bill of Materials.
