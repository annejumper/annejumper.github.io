I use SolidWorks for part modeling and assemblies in my coursework and projects. These are a few models that each practiced a different set of techniques:

- **Lego brick:** exact dimensions with patterned studs and the hollow underside with its tubes, so the part would actually mate with a real brick.
- **Juicer assembly:** a multi-part lever press built with mates for the linkage, handle and cone, so the mechanism moves through its range.
- **Hammer head:** lofts and fillets to blend the flat claw into the round striking face.
- **Candlestick holder:** a revolved profile for the body, with a swept handle added to the base.
- **Support base:** a machined-style bracket with holes, a counterbore and an angled gusset, modeled with manufacturing in mind.
- **Pumpkin:** organic surfaces from a revolved body with patterned ribs and a swept stem.
